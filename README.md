This recipe is a work in progress. It is made using a mix of learning,
love, health, and patience. Results may vary, but consistency is the
secret ingredient.


- 1 supportive girlfriend (Tulika)
- Daily DSA practice
- Daily Odin Project lessons
- Good sleep
- A little exercise
- Family time
- Patience
- Self-respect
- Lots of laughter

STEPS 


1. Wake up and avoid checking your phone immediately.

2. Spend some time learning something new
   (DSA, Web Development, or anything useful).

3. Talk to Tulika and genuinely listen to her,
   not just reply.

4. Solve one problem at a time instead of worrying
   about your entire future.

5. Take care of your health and move your body.

6. Be kind to people you care about.

7. Don't compare your journey with everyone else's.

8. End the day by appreciating one thing that went well.

9. Repeat daily and adjust ingredients when necessary.
